# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Contributors must engage
constructively, assume good faith while testing technical claims, and focus criticism on ideas and
work rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, or sustained
disruption of project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation that
violates these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent
ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `engineering@inttegro.com`. Maintainers will protect the
privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible and will disclose conflicts of
interest when choosing who handles a report.

This policy applies in repository discussions, community spaces, and public interactions when a
person is representing Cogni or Inttegro.
